Jarvis

He appears in the room with you, in passthrough, on a Meta Quest or through your phone’s camera. Say “Hey Jarvis” and he answers.

No pairing and no microphone needed. Place a few made-up things — a kitchen light, a TV, an inbox — then press Done and he arrives: select him to see him speak, listen and think, and point at a thing (on a phone, tap it) to see it picked and lit as if he were working on it. The made-up things are kept apart from the real ones.

Put what Jarvis works on — a light, an inbox, a calendar — where it is in your room. It lights up while he works on it, and you can point at it and say “that”. No pairing and no microphone needed; in the room, A or X, or the button on the back of your wrist, opens it too.

When the headset can keep his voice out of its own microphone, you hear him from his spot in the room. Otherwise, or with this off, you hear him from the headset.

Your Jarvis server

Jarvis talks to you through your own Jarvis server. Pair this headset with it once, and the server starts his conversations: no ElevenLabs key is typed here or kept in this browser. Pairing goes through your server's sign-in, if it has one; after that the headset needs no sign-in to talk to him.

Sign in to your server, then press Pair again.

No headset? See him on a desktop.
