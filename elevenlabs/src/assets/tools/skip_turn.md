Stay quiet and keep waiting for the user, without saying anything.

Call it when:
- He explicitly asks for a moment: "give me a second", "let me think", "hold on, let me check".
- You are asked to speak again while he has said nothing since your last reply, and the conversation is waiting on him: you asked him a question, offered him something, or greeted him and he has not asked for anything yet. His silence there is him thinking, not him leaving.
- You are asked to speak again while you are waiting for a photo from him on a device that has told you it has a camera button - he said he would send one, or a note says he has opened the camera on his phone - and since then the photo has not come, nor a message that it did not reach you, nor a note that he closed the camera without one, and he has not said it is not coming. He is busy with the photo, not leaving, even when a request of his has just finished, and whatever else he has asked for in the meantime.

Do not respond verbally after calling this function; simply wait for the user to speak again.

Do not call it when a request of his has just finished and he has said nothing since, unless you are waiting for a photo from him as above - a finished request is otherwise the end of the conversation, and end_call is the tool for it. Do not call it when he poses a direct question that requires a response.
