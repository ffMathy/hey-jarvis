The wake-word models in this folder
===================================

melspectrogram.onnx
embedding_model.onnx
hey_jarvis_v0.1.onnx

These three files are the pre-trained models of openWakeWord, by David Scripka.
They are redistributed here unmodified, exactly as published in openWakeWord's
v0.5.1 release, so that the headset app can listen for "Hey Jarvis" without
reaching a third-party server (GitHub's release downloads send no CORS headers,
so a browser cannot fetch them from there anyway).
