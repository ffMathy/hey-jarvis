Jarvis

He appears in the room with you, in passthrough, on a Meta Quest or through your phone’s camera. Say “Hey Jarvis” and he answers.

No key and no microphone needed. Place a few made-up things — a kitchen light, a TV, an inbox — then press Done and he arrives: select him to see him speak, listen and think, and point at a thing (on a phone, tap it) to see it picked and lit as if he were working on it. The made-up things are kept apart from the real ones.

Put what Jarvis works on — a light, an inbox, a calendar — where it is in your room. It lights up while he works on it, and you can point at it and say “that”. No key and no microphone needed; in the room, A or X, or the button on the back of your wrist, opens it too.

When the headset can keep his voice out of its own microphone, you hear him from his spot in the room. Otherwise, or with this off, you hear him from the headset.

If Cloudflare Zero Trust is in front of your server, sign in to it once in this browser. Jarvis connects on his own within half a minute of signing in.

Your ElevenLabs agent

Jarvis is an ElevenLabs agent. The API key is kept in this browser's local storage, which is not a keystore: anything running on this site can read it. Make a key for Jarvis alone.
